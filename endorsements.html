People who taught me, supervised my theses, or examined my work directly.

Master's Supervisor
He chose an ambitious research agenda, original methodological work in causal inference, and pursued it with a discipline and pace that allowed him to complete his thesis ahead of the customary timeline. […] Mr. Hugo Gobato Souto is in the top 2% of the most capable researchers I have supervised out of the dozens of students I have taught or supervised at USP.
